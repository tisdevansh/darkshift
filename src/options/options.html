Welcome to DarkShift 🌙

Every website is now dark. Click the DarkShift icon in your toolbar to switch a tab, a site or all sites between Dark, Filter and Off, and to tune brightness, contrast and blue light.

Tip: pin the extension (puzzle icon → pin) so it's always one click away. Already-open tabs were converted automatically.

General

DarkShift enabled
Master switch for every tab. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+P
Default mode for all sites
Used unless a site rule or a tab override says otherwise.
DarkRewrites the page's own colors into a dark palette. Keeps images, videos and colored highlights (like green "correct" answers) intact. Best quality.
FilterInverts the whole page and flips images back. Use it for canvas-heavy apps or the rare site Dark can't handle.
OffLeaves the page exactly as designed.
